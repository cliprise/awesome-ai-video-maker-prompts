## Why most AI video prompts fail

Weak prompt:

```text
Make a cool AI video.
```

This usually creates random motion.

Better prompt:

```text
Create an 8-second vertical product video of a matte black wireless headphone case opening slowly on a reflective dark glass surface. Camera: slow push-in from a low angle. Lighting: soft blue rim light with premium studio reflections. Style: realistic technology commercial, minimal, polished. Motion: case opens smoothly, light sweeps across the surface, background stays stable. Restrictions: no text, no logo distortion, no extra objects, no flicker.
```

The better prompt works because it defines:

- duration
- format
- subject
- action
- setting
- camera movement
- lighting
- style
- motion
- restrictions

## The AI video maker prompt formula

Use this structure for most AI video generation tasks:

```text
Create a [duration]-second [format] video of [main subject] doing [clear action] in [environment].
Camera: [movement, framing, angle].
Lighting: [lighting direction and mood].
Style: [realistic, cinematic, social, commercial, anime, documentary, etc.].
Motion: [what moves, how fast, what stays stable].
Restrictions: [no text, no logo distortion, no extra limbs, no flicker, no warped geometry].
```

Example:

```text
Create a 7-second vertical video of a handmade ceramic coffee mug on a wooden table beside a window. Camera: slow push-in from a close-up angle. Lighting: soft morning light with warm shadows. Style: realistic lifestyle product ad, calm and premium. Motion: steam rises gently, camera moves slowly, mug stays stable. Restrictions: no text, no extra objects, no logo distortion, no flicker.
```

## Text-to-video vs image-to-video

### Use text-to-video when:

- you want concept exploration
- exact identity does not matter
- you need a new scene from scratch
- you are testing creative directions
- you want cinematic imagination
- you do not already have a source image

### Use image-to-video when:

- product consistency matters
- character consistency matters
- brand mascot consistency matters
- you already have a strong image
- you want controlled motion
- you want ad variations from one product shot
- you want to animate AI art or a thumbnail

## Common AI video maker mistakes

### Mistake 1: Asking for a full story in one generation

AI video works better with short, clear scenes.

### Mistake 2: Too much motion

Too many moving elements can create flicker, distortion or chaos.

### Mistake 3: No camera direction

Camera movement matters. Specify push-in, orbit, tracking, close-up, wide shot or stable framing.

### Mistake 4: No format planning

A TikTok clip and a YouTube video need different framing.

### Mistake 5: Asking for readable text

Generated video text may be unreliable. Add text later when accuracy matters.

### Mistake 6: Ignoring image-to-video

If consistency matters, create a source image first and animate it.

### Mistake 7: Not saving winning prompts

Every successful prompt should become a reusable template.

## FAQ

### What is an AI video maker?

An AI video maker is a tool or workflow that helps create videos using artificial intelligence. It can generate video from text prompts, animate images, create short clips, edit existing video or help build social media and marketing videos.

### What is an AI video creator?

An AI video creator usually refers to a creator-friendly AI video workflow for making social clips, ads, YouTube Shorts, TikTok videos, product videos and other content.

### How do I create AI videos?

Start with a specific goal, choose text-to-video or image-to-video, write a clear prompt with subject, action, camera, lighting, style and restrictions, generate controlled variations, then review and polish the best result.

### Can I make AI videos for free?

Some platforms offer free credits, trials or limited access. Free workflows are useful for testing and learning, but production work may require credits, better models, editing, upscaling and export control.

### What is the best prompt for an AI video maker?

A good prompt includes duration, format, subject, action, environment, camera movement, lighting, style, motion and restrictions.

### What is an AI clip maker?

An AI clip maker creates short video clips for social media, ads, intros, product teasers or campaign assets. Short clips usually work best when the prompt has one clear subject and one clear motion.

### What is an AI movie maker?

An AI movie maker workflow uses AI to create cinematic shots, storyboards or concept scenes. It is usually better to create short scenes one by one instead of asking for a full movie in one prompt.

### What is the difference between text-to-video and image-to-video?

Text-to-video starts from a written prompt. Image-to-video starts from an uploaded image and adds motion. Image-to-video is often better when consistency matters.

### What makes a good AI video prompt?

A good prompt gives the model a clear visual brief: duration, subject, action, camera, lighting, style, motion and restrictions.

### How do I avoid bad AI video results?

Use shorter scenes, simpler motion, stable camera movement, clear subjects, reference images when consistency matters and specific restrictions for common artifacts.

### Can AI video be used for marketing?

Yes. AI video can be useful for product ads, concept testing, social media clips, app promos, landing page visuals, explainer scenes and campaign variations.

### Can AI video replace traditional production?

Not always. AI video is strongest for fast creative testing, short-form assets, concepting and scalable content variations. For high-stakes production, it often works best as part of a broader workflow.
